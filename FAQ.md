## 仍有问题？
***如果你的母语是简体中文，请阅读这个板块。***

1. 本协议与 CC BY-NC-SA 或 PolyForm Noncommercial 的区别在哪？  
   显而易见，这二者均比 SCUL 更专业，引入风险也更少。  
   除非你有一些~~怪癖~~特殊需求，否则不建议在正式项目中使用 SCUL。  
   并且自述文件也讲得很清楚了，“你不应把 SCUL 比作任何其他许可证”。

2. 既然如此，我在什么情况下才应该引入 SCUL？  
   在任何情况下都不，因为你说的是“应该”。  
   但如果你引入 SCUL 的初衷仅仅是自己想这么做，而且同我一样对非商业有近乎偏执的追求，那或许可以在一些练手的项目里试试。

3. 与 GPL 等开源许可证的兼容性？  
   完全没有。都说了是非开源许可证了。

4. 我一开始使用的是 SCUL，后来后悔了，想改成 MIT，来得及吗？  
   这就是程序员的魅力了——你可以随时改动你的许可证，只要法律和用户愿意的话。  
   不保证未来 SCUL 会不会出现显式排他的条款。反正我的 todo 里没有。

5. 我一开始使用的是 SCUL 的较早版本，后来想升级了，可以吗？

6. SCUL 是谁写的？有专业律师背书吗？

## FAQ
***If your native language is English, please read this section.***  
Before attempting to introduce SCUL into your project (which is, of course, highly discouraged), you may have some questions.  
If so, you can try to find answers to some common questions here.

1. What is the difference between this license and CC BY-NC-SA or PolyForm Noncommercial?  
   Obviously, both are more professional than SCUL and carry less risk to adopt.  
   Unless you have some ~~quirks~~ special needs, SCUL is not recommended for formal projects.  
   And the README makes it very clear: “You should not compare SCUL to any other license.”

2. That being the case, under what circumstances should I introduce SCUL?  
   Under none, because you said “should.”  
   But if your reason for introducing SCUL is simply that you want to, and, like me, you have an almost obsessive commitment to noncommercial use, then perhaps you can try it in some practice projects.

3. Compatibility with open-source licenses such as GPL?  
   None at all. Like I said, it’s a non-open-source license.

4. I initially used SCUL, then regretted it and want to change to MIT. Is it too late?  
   That’s the charm of being a programmer—you can change your license at any time, as long as the law and your users are willing.  
   There’s no guarantee whether SCUL will have explicitly exclusionary clauses in the future. Anyway, it’s not on my to-do list.

5. I initially used an earlier version of SCUL, and later want to upgrade. Is that okay?  
   Not to v2; other versions are fine.

6. Who wrote SCUL? Is it endorsed by a professional lawyer?  
   I don’t think the first question is something any normal person would ask. Unless there are contributors in the future, that is.  
   As for a professional lawyer’s endorsement, of course\*2 there is one—if AI counts. ~~I’m not a law major, okay?!~~

7. Is there anything I can do for SCUL?  
   Just help spread the word, point out bugs to me, submit a PR, or something like that.  
   Of course\*3, if you really do that, I’ll definitely kowtow to you :D

8. Are there any risks in using SCUL?  
   Of course\*4. For example, you may lose ownership of your code, and you may also be hit with a fine.  
   Of course\*5, it depends on your local laws; after all, I already said I didn’t study law.

9. What distribution license does SCUL use?  
   SCUL itself. Bet you didn’t expect that.

10. Will this file be expanded in the future?  
    Probably not. So if you have any other questions, send me an email.
